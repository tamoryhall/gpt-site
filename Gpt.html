Drag-and-Drop Algebra Worksheet

Goal: Drag the correct label into each blank.

Students will identify:

  • Term = a part of an expression separated by + or −
  • Coefficient = the number multiplying a variable
  • Constant = a number with no variable
Example: In 4x + 7, the coefficient is 4, the constant is 7, and 4x is a term.
